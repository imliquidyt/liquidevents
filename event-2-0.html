Event 2.0 - Atlantis vs Aether

Two civilizations. Two worlds. This page contains all details for Event 2.0 only.

All listed dates are tentative and NOT confirmed Session timing confirmed: 7pm-11pm GMT Applications closed until further notice

Important: Schedule dates below are planning dates only and may change at any time.

Concept

  • Atlantis starts in an underwater dimension with water breathing.
  • Atlantis can explore custom ocean biomes and structures.
  • Atlantis cannot leave the ocean until Day 4.
  • Aether starts in a sky dimension inspired by Aether-style gameplay.
  • Aether explores floating islands, custom biomes, and structures.
