Event 2.0 - Atlantis vs Aether

Two civilizations. Two worlds. This page contains all official details for Event 2.0 only.

Officially Happening 7pm-11pm GMT Confirmed Player Applications Open

Official update: Event 2.0 is officially happening. Session dates and timings are confirmed.

Concept

  • Atlantis: starts in an underwater dimension with water breathing enabled.
  • Atlantis players can explore custom ocean biomes and structures.
  • Atlantis cannot leave the ocean dimension until Day 4.
  • Aether: starts in a sky dimension inspired by Aether-style gameplay.
  • Aether players explore floating islands, custom biomes, and structures.
