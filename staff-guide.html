Purpose of This Guide

This guide exists to:

  • Set clear expectations for staff
  • Prevent abuse and favoritism
  • Ensure fairness
  • Protect the integrity of the event
  • Maintain professionalism
⚠️ All staff members must follow this guide at all times. Failure to follow it may result in demotion or permanent removal.
👥

Zero Tolerance: Admin Abuse

Admin Abuse includes any of the following:

  • Giving items
  • Placing blocks
  • Removing blocks
  • Teleporting players unfairly
  • Revealing locations
  • Spawning mobs
  • Changing weather/time
  • Editing inventories
  • Warning friends unfairly
  • Ignoring rule breaks
🚫 NOT EVEN ONE DIRT BLOCK MAY BE PLACED.

Punishment:

  • First Offense: Immediate Suspension
  • Second Offense: Permanent Removal + Public Announcement

  • Rule Broken
  • Evidence Link
  • Punishment Given

Staff Training: How to Handle Rule Breaks

1
Observe
  • Do NOT act immediately
  • Watch carefully
  • Start recording
2
Collect Evidence
  • Screenshot or video
  • Chat logs
  • Coordinates (if needed)
3
Verify
  • Check rules
  • Confirm violation
  • Ask higher staff if unsure
4
Act Fairly
  • Apply correct punishment
  • No emotions
  • No favoritism
5
Log
  • Post in #punishment-logs
  • Attach proof

How to Be a Professional Staff Member

All staff are expected to:

  • Stay calm in all situations
  • Never argue with players
  • Never insult players
  • Explain rules clearly
  • Give players a chance to understand
  • Be consistent
  • Ask for help when unsure
  • Protect server reputation

Things Staff Must NEVER Do

  • Threaten players
  • Mock players
  • Abuse commands
  • Leak private info
  • Take sides in wars
  • Punish without proof
  • Ignore appeals
⚠️ Violation = Demotion or Removal
📌

Appeals & Reviews

Players may appeal through tickets. Staff must:

  • Review fairly
  • Re-check evidence
  • Involve managers
  • Update logs if reversed
🚫 Fake denial of appeals = Removal
